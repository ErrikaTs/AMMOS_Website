Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About AMMOS

Browse all 5 categories below or filter by topic to explore answers on platform matching, Crew access, Captain plans, Founding Member billing, and data privacy.

Category 1

General

AMMOS is a real-time yachting recruitment platform that connects Captains directly with Crew members. Captains post job listings or send direct crew invites, Crew members apply to positions matching their role and location, and once both sides match, full profiles and instant in-app chat unlock automatically.

To protect personal privacy and prevent unsolicited spam, full crew profiles (including CV, certificates, and contact details) and direct messaging remain private until a mutual match occurs (Full profile on match and Chat on match). A match happens when a Captain accepts a Crew member's job application or when a Crew member accepts a Captain's direct invite.

No. AMMOS is strictly a technology platform that facilitates direct connections between Captains and Crew. AMMOS does not act as an employer, maritime recruiter, or crewing agency, and does not participate in employment contracts, salary negotiations, or onboard operations. Captains and Crew are solely responsible for verifying maritime certificates, licenses, references, visas, and legal eligibility before entering into any employment agreement.

Category 2

For Crew Members

Yes. Creating a Crew profile, uploading your CV and certificates, browsing job listings, receiving priority job alerts, applying for positions, and chatting with matched Captains is 100% free for Crew members.

When you set your status to available and enable location permissions and push notifications, AMMOS matches you with nearby opportunities. Whenever a Captain posts an Urgent listing within a 150 km radius of your location, you receive an instant priority alert so you can review and apply right away.

Category 3

For Captains — Plans, Priority & Urgent Listings

  • • Free ($0): 1 yacht, 1 job listing per month, Standard search & alert priority, plus full crew profile and chat on match.
  • • Pro ($49/mo or $490/yr): 1 yacht, Unlimited listings, 3 Urgent listings per month, Unlimited crew invites, and High priority.
  • • Premium ($149/mo or $1490/yr): Unlimited yachts, Unlimited listings, Unlimited Urgent listings, Unlimited crew invites, and Top (1st) priority.

Job listings and crew notifications are ranked hierarchically so subscribed Captains fill positions faster. All Urgent listings are pinned at the top of Search and Home first (ordered by Premium ➔ Pro ➔ Free), followed by standard listings ordered by subscription tier (Premium Top 1st ➔ Pro High ➔ Free Standard).

An Urgent listing pins your job 1st in Search & Home, expands visibility to a 3× wider radius (150 km), and sends an instant priority alert to available crew. If you are on the Free plan or have used your 3 monthly Urgent listings on Pro, you can purchase a single Extra Urgent Listing for $9.99 / job as a one-time, non-recurring purchase without changing your subscription.

Category 4

Free Trial, Billing, Founding Members & Cancellations

Yes. New Captains subscribing to Pro or Premium for the first time receive a 30-day free trial (available once per account lifetime). You can explore all plan features free for 30 days, and you will only be billed if you choose to keep your subscription active after the 30-day trial period ends.

When you choose Yearly (2 months free), you are billed once per year at a discounted annual rate equal to 10 months instead of 12, giving you 2 full months free every year compared to monthly billing.

All subscriptions and payments are processed securely by Apple (App Store on iOS) or Google (Google Play on Android). Your subscription automatically renews at the end of each billing cycle (monthly or yearly) unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of your current trial or billing period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your device's Apple ID or Google Play Subscriptions settings, or via Profile ➔ Choose Your Plan inside AMMOS.

Sign into your AMMOS account on your new device, go to Profile ➔ Choose Your Plan, and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the screen to sync your active subscription.

Captains who subscribe to Pro or Premium during our first month of launch lock in special Founding Member rates ($29/mo or $290/yr for Pro, and $89/mo or $890/yr for Premium) for life, as long as their paid subscription remains active. Founding Members can switch freely between Pro and Premium or between Monthly and Yearly billing while keeping their discount, but downgrading to Free or canceling permanently forfeits the Founding Member rate.

Category 5

Privacy, Data Collection, Account Deletion & Safety

You can permanently delete your account at any time directly inside the app by going to Profile ➔ Delete Account and confirming the deletion. Deleting your account immediately removes your public profile, CV, yachts, active job listings, applications, and chat access.

If you have already deleted the AMMOS app from your device, you can request full account and data deletion by emailing our support team at support@ammosyachting.com from the email address registered to your AMMOS account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will permanently delete your account and personal data within 7 business days (excluding only transaction invoices required to be retained by tax law). Note: Uninstalling the app or deleting your AMMOS account does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription; please ensure you also cancel your subscription in your Apple or Google account settings.

In compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play Data Safety standards, AMMOS collects only the data necessary to operate the recruitment platform:

  • • Account & Contact Info: Name, email, phone number, and authentication credentials to create and secure your account.
  • • Professional Profile & Yacht Data: Role, sea-time experience, certificates, CV documents, profile photos, and yacht specifications to match Captains and Crew.
  • • Location Data: Approximate or precise location (when permitted) to display nearby jobs and deliver 150 km Urgent job alerts.
  • • In-App Communications: Job applications, crew invites, and chat messages between matched users.
  • • Subscription & Device Identifiers: Purchase status (via Apple/Google) and push notification tokens to deliver real-time alerts.

All data is encrypted in transit, used strictly for app functionality, and is never sold to third-party advertisers.

AMMOS maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, fake listings, discrimination, or abusive behavior. You can report or block any user, job listing, or chat conversation directly inside the app using the Report/Block option on their profile or chat screen, or by contacting support@ammosyachting.com. Reported content is reviewed by our moderation team within 24 hours.
