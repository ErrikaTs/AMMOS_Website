Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About AMMOS

Click any question below to expand detailed answers on port-local scheduling, CV privacy, nominal certifications, and account management.

Every job posted on AMMOS is automatically anchored to the departure port’s local timezone (via Google Places timezone lookup) rather than the viewer’s phone clock. Once a job’s scheduled port-local start time passes by more than 20 minutes (+20m cutoff), it automatically hides from public Crew search results so Crew only see live, actionable shifts.

To protect Crew privacy, uploaded CVs/Resumes and direct phone numbers are strictly locked while browsing candidates or reviewing initial applications. They unlock exclusively when a mutual Active Match is established between the Captain and Crew member for a specific job.

Crew members can tap the Calendar view on their Home or Profile tab to toggle available dates on or off in seconds. Captains can manage their fleet under “My Yachts” using visual calendar-style vessel cards that store yacht name, length, type, home port, and upcoming shift schedules.

AMMOS allows Crew to select standard maritime qualifications (such as STCW, ENG1, PBL2, or Yachtmaster) nominally for fast filtering and matching without storing sensitive government-issued certificate scans on our servers. Captains verify original physical certificates directly with Crew upon onboarding.

Ratings can only be submitted after an Active Match job has been completed. Both Captain and Crew can leave a verified 1-to-5 star rating, and a profile’s displayed rating is the live mathematical average of all completed job reviews.

AMMOS Pro subscriptions are billed and managed directly through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings, where you can manage or cancel auto-renewal at any time. To permanently delete your AMMOS account and personal data, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account in the mobile app, or use our Account & Data Deletion Portal.
