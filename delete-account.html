Account & Personal Data Deletion

Users of AMMOS can permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time—either instantaneously inside the mobile app or by submitting the web deletion request form below.

Option A · Instant Self-Service

Instant In-App Deletion

For immediate removal of your profile, yachts, job history, uploaded CV, and chat threads, follow these 4 steps inside the AMMOS app:

  1. 1

    Open the AMMOS mobile app and sign in.

    Launch AMMOS on your iOS or Android device using your registered Captain or Crew credentials.

  2. 2

    Go to the “Profile” tab in the bottom bar.

    Select the rightmost Profile icon on the persistent 5-tab navigation bar.

  3. 3

    Tap the Settings icon in the top-right corner.

    Open your account preferences and privacy management screen.

  4. 4

    Tap “Delete Account” and confirm permanent deletion.

    Confirm the prompt to permanently erase your account and associated personal data.

Option B · Web Portal Request

Web Account Deletion Request Form

If you have already uninstalled the AMMOS app, submit a verified deletion request below and our Data Safety team will process it.

You may also email your deletion request directly to support@blueeyeluxury.com.
